Three Types of Chocolate

  • Dark Chocolate
  • Bittersweet Chocolate
  • Milk Chocolate

Dark Chocolate

I've recently taken a liking to dark chocolate. It's a super food, and goes well with Greek yoghurt, and granola.

Quick Facts

  • Dark chocolate typically ranges from 50% to 90% cacao.
  • It is made primarily of cacao beans and sugar, and traditionally contains no milk solids.
  • It contains higher levels of flavanols, iron, and magnesium compared to milk chocolate.

Bittersweet Chocolate

It's good for cookies!!

Quick Facts

  • Bittersweet chocolate usually features a cacao percentage of around 70%.
  • It provides a deep, robust chocolate taste with less sugar and a slightly drier, crumblier texture than sweeter varieties.
  • It is a preferred choice for baking applications like brownies, tarts, and fudgy bar cookies.

Milk Chocolate

Milk chocolate. Ah, the best of the best. You know, one of the first chocolates I ever had in my life was Carlos V from Mexico.

Quick Facts

  • Milk chocolate generally contains a lower concentration of cacao, typically landing between 10% and 50%.
  • It incorporates milk solids into the formula to create a sweeter, milder flavor profile.
  • The presence of milk fat allows it to melt more softly and coat the palate longer.
